Zaid Amedi Company is a general trading and contracting company operating across Iraq and Turkey, with offices in Duhok, Erbil, Mosul and Istanbul.

We specialize in mechanical and electrical solutions for large-scale projects, supplying modern HVAC technologies, fire protection systems, and reliable power generation equipment.

Our expertise includes VRV / VRF systems and chiller solutions from global brands including Daikin, LG and SKM.
